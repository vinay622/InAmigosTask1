The Genesis

Founded on a Vision of Change

InAmigos Foundation was founded on September 23, 2020, by Mr. Govind Shukla (Founder & CEO). Operating from our base in Chhattisgarh, we recognized a profound need for structured, transparent, and compassionate intervention in society's most vulnerable sectors.

We believe in the power of collective action. Every contribution, whether time, resource, or advocacy, is directed towards essential causes such as food distribution, education, women empowerment, animal welfare, environmental sustainability, and social inclusion programs across India.

"Together, we can create a more inclusive, compassionate, and empowered society."
